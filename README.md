# Valentina Becherucci

**Clinical Laboratory Biologist | Laboratory Medicine | Hematology | Biomedical Research**

I am a clinical laboratory biologist working in hospital laboratory medicine, with experience spanning hematology, coagulation, clinical chemistry, urinalysis, flow cytometry, cell therapy, and biomedical research.

My current scientific interests include laboratory hematology, morphology-assisted interpretation, non-blood biological fluids, analytical method comparison, and the application of artificial intelligence to laboratory medicine and clinical decision-support systems.
